Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 27, 2023


Beijing Youche Car Service Co., Ltd ("Company", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal data of our users globally. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding data collection, usage, and age-related protections for our mobile applications and games.

1. Privacy Data Adaptation & Collection

We collect information to provide better services to all our users. The data we adapt and collect includes:

  • Device Information: We collect device-specific information (such as hardware model, OS version, unique device identifiers like IDFA/GAID, and mobile network information) to optimize performance and for advertising attribution.
  • Usage Data: We track how you interact with our apps, including features used, levels achieved, and in-app purchase history, to improve user experience.
  • Location Data: We may collect coarse location data (IP address) to provide localized content and comply with regional regulations.
  • Personal Identifiers: If you contact support or register an account, we may collect your name and email address solely for communication purposes.

2. Global Age Management & Policy

We are dedicated to the protection of children's privacy. Our services are adapted to global age standards:

  • COPPA (USA): We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without verifiable parental consent. If we discover a child under 13 has provided us with personal info, we delete it immediately.
  • GDPR-K (EU): For users in the EEA, the age of digital consent varies by country (typically 13-16). We provide enhanced privacy protections for minors in these regions.
  • Age Rating: All our products are clearly labeled with age ratings (ESRB, PEGI, IARC) on the Google Play and App Store to help parents make informed decisions.

3. Third-Party Monetization & Analytics

To provide free-to-play services, we partner with industry-leading platforms. These partners may use SDKs to collect data for ad-targeting and analytics:

  • Ad Partners: Google AdMob, Meta Audience Network, Unity Ads, AppLovin.
  • Analytics: Firebase, AppsFlyer, Google Analytics.

We ensure these partners comply with Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) and Google's Data Safety requirements.

4. Data Security & Storage

We implement robust technical and organizational measures to safeguard your data against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration. Data is stored on secure cloud servers with restricted access.

5. Contact Our Privacy Officer

For any privacy-related inquiries or to exercise your data rights (access, deletion, portability), please contact us at:

Email: support@bjyouche.com
